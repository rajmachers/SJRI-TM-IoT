Executive Summary & Next-Gen Expansion Roadmap

What went great: 11/11 assets reported location data, with 12,629 meaningful location transitions captured since Jan 2026 and strong repeat utilization in OPD and billing corridors.
Operational gaps identified: 6,978 meaningful transitions still ended in unknown zones, and 2 assets had no meaningful movement for >24h (1 asset >7 days), indicating parked/maintenance or process bottlenecks.
Immediate PoC hardening (0-30 days): prioritize unknown-anchor mapping (top 4 anchors), introduce end-of-day asset assembly reconciliation, and create exception queues for parked assets by no-movement bands (>24h, >72h, >7d).
Next-gen Asset + Porter platform (30-90 days): add digital request-book-assign workflows, SLA timers, and closed-loop confirmation so equipment movement is demand-driven and auditable.
Clinical rapid response highlight: BLE-based proximity lookup for Code Blue can identify nearest eligible asset/personnel faster, supporting SJRI expectation for emergency readiness and safer asset utilization.
Management KPI set for scale: meaningful mobility rate, unknown-zone share, parked-asset ratio, retrieval SLA adherence, and day-close reconciliation compliance by department.
